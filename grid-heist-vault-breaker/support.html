Support & help

Last updated: September 6, 2026

For Grid Heist Vault Breaker / Nexus Breaker: Core Architect support and feedback, contact exaque2@gmail.com.

Prepare a support email

Your email app opens; you complete sending. No support request is submitted automatically.

When reporting a problem

Include the game version, device model, operating system, level and steps to reproduce. You may include a screenshot with personal information removed. We do not ask for passwords, sign-in codes or payment details.

A piece will not fit

The entire piece must fit in empty cells. Try rotation, a free hint or reserve. If dragging is difficult, choose tap placement under Hub → Settings (Merkez → Ayarlar); select a target, then confirm.

My progress did not follow my account

In this version, signing in does not sync progress between devices. Do not clear app data on the device containing your save. Cloud backup is not yet available.

An ad or reward did not work

An ad may be unavailable because of connectivity, consent eligibility or the test ad service. An unavailable ad does not grant a reward. If a reward is missing or the screen keeps waiting, tell support your device/version and whether you selected life or continue. Free practice and natural life regeneration remain available.

How do I delete my account or data?

Open the account and data deletion page. Removing an account identity and resetting the local save are separate actions. You can request deletion by email even without the app.
