RuneSequence Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026 · Publisher: CodExa · Contact: exaque2@gmail.com

RuneSequence is a standalone number-rule puzzle game built by CodExa. Playing the 60-page folio and Daily Folio does not require registration, a profile, or cloud sync.

1. On-Device Storage

Your solved rules, Codex entries, Daily Folio history, preferences (language, sound, haptics), and cached Premium entitlement remain on your device in local platform storage (UserDefaults on iOS and SharedPreferences on Android). RuneSequence does not transmit your puzzle history to CodExa servers.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Players who have not unlocked Premium may see chapter-completion interstitial ads and may choose to watch rewarded video ads for Hint Tiers 2 and 3. Google AdMob may process device identifiers, coarse location, and ad-interaction telemetry according to your consent selections (Google UMP and Apple App Tracking Transparency) and Google's Privacy Policy. Where required by regional privacy law, you can revisit ad consent choices inside in-app Settings.

3. Store Billing (In-App Purchases)

The one-time Premium upgrade (com.codexa.runesequence.premium) and purchase restoration are handled exclusively by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. CodExa never receives payment card or billing details.

4. Optional Version Check

When an internet connection is present, the app may query Apple's iTunes Lookup API or Google Play In-App Updates to check whether a newer release is published. The app adds no personal data to this lookup request; standard network metadata (such as IP address) is handled by Apple or Google under their respective privacy policies.

5. First-Party Analytics

Version 1.0 of RuneSequence does not include a first-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

6. Audience & Contact

RuneSequence is designed for audiences aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Questions or requests may be sent to exaque2@gmail.com.
