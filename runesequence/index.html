Copperplate Number-Rule Puzzle

RuneSequence

Most number puzzles ask for the next number. RuneSequence asks you to forge the rule that creates it using runic tiles (Prev, Two back, n, +, -, ×, 1-9) to shatter the Sierpinski copperplate.

20 Fork Pages
