Contact

Restoring Your Premium Upgrade

Tap the page badge at the top-left of the screen to open the Folio Picker, scroll to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account used for the original purchase.

How Evaluation Works

RuneSequence evaluates every rule strictly from left to right (for example, 2 + 3 × 4 = 20) and displays parentheses automatically as you place tiles. Every valid rule that generates all shown terms is accepted.
