Visual Timer for iPhone & iPad

See your time. Find your focus.

A visual countdown disk, precise time entry, Pomodoro work and break phases, themes, completion chimes and synthesized ambient audio. No app account required. The timer works offline.

Free with banner ads. Optional Lifetime Pro removes ads and unlocks premium themes and audio for one payment. No subscriptions. US base price: USD 4.99; local prices are shown by Apple.
