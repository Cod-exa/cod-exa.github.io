# Privacy Policy for Visual Timer

**Effective date:** July 5, 2026  
**Developer:** CodExa  
**Contact:** [exaque2@gmail.com](mailto:exaque2@gmail.com)  

---

### The short version
Visual Timer works fully offline. We never ask for your name, email, or any account. We collect no personal information ourselves. The free version uses Google AdMob (one ad after a timer ends) and Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics, which collect limited device data described below. Buying Pro removes ads.

---

### 1. Data we do NOT collect
We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera data, or any content you create. Timer durations, routines, and settings are stored only on your device and never leave it.

### 2. Data collected by third-party services
The free version includes the following Google services:

* **Google AdMob** (advertising — free version only). Shows a single ad on the post-timer summary screen. AdMob may collect: device advertising identifier, IP address (coarse location), device model and OS version, and ad interaction data. Ads are configured as non-personalized by default.  
  AdMob privacy: [https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
* **Firebase Analytics** (usage statistics). Collects anonymous app events (e.g., "timer started", "purchase completed"), device model, OS version, and a random app-instance identifier. We use this only to understand which features are used.  
  Firebase privacy: [https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
* **Firebase Crashlytics** (stability). Collects crash logs and device state at the moment of a crash so we can fix bugs.
* **Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase**. Purchases are processed entirely by Google/Apple. We never see your payment details.

### 3. Children's privacy
Visual Timer is intended for adults — teachers, parents, and individuals managing their own focus. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app requires no account and stores no personal content, and ads are non-personalized. If you believe a child has provided personal data through our services, contact us and we will delete it. (COPPA / GDPR-K)

### 4. Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, KVKK)
Because we hold no personal data on our servers, most requests (access, deletion, portability) can be fulfilled by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. For data held by Google (AdMob/Firebase identifiers), you can:
* Reset your advertising ID in device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking)
* Contact us at [exaque2@gmail.com](mailto:exaque2@gmail.com) for any privacy request — we respond within 30 days
* EU/UK users: you may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority
* Türkiye: KVKK kapsamındaki başvurular için aynı e-posta adresi geçerlidir

### 5. Data retention & security
On-device data stays until you delete the app or clear its storage. Firebase analytics data is retained by Google for a limited period (default 14 months), then deleted automatically. All third-party transmissions use encryption in transit (TLS).

### 6. Changes to this policy
We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
