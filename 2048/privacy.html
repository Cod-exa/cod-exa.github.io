2. Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while playing, we use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Below is the list of services we use and the data they collect:

Firebase Authentication (Identity Management)

If you register or log in using Google Sign-In or Email/Password, Firebase Authentication secures your credentials and provides a unique User ID. This allows your score and premium settings to be saved securely. No authentication data is shared with other third parties.

Google AdMob (Advertising)

Our app serves ads using Google AdMob. To serve relevant, personalized, or non-personalized advertisements, AdMob collects details including:

  • Advertising ID (Ad ID)
  • Device information and manufacturer models
  • IP address and coarse location
  • Ad interaction and impression statistics

We use the mandatory AD_ID permission in our manifest to support advertising attribution and SDK performance.

Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases)

When you purchase the Premium Ad-Free upgrade, Google Play handles all transaction details. Our app only receives a receipt token to verify your purchase and unlock the feature. We do not store or collect credit card details or financial information.
