How to play

01

Build your deck

Create a 30-card deck. Balance mana, attack, health and card abilities.

02

Enter the battlefield

Spend your growing mana each turn. Drag a unit onto your field and choose a valid target for its ability.

03

Finish your move

Attack with your units, use your hero power and reduce the opposing hero’s 30 health to zero.
