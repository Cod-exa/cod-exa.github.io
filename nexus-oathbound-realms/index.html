Nexus: Oathbound Realms

Make your oath.
Change the battlefield.

A fantasy strategy game with 20 lineages and 220 cards. The strength of your deck matters—and so does the moment you choose to play each card.

In development · Android test build

How do you play?Get support

How do you play?

01

Build your deck

Create a 30-card deck. Balance mana, attack, health and card abilities.

02

Enter the battlefield

Spend your growing mana each turn. Drag a unit onto your field and choose a valid target for its ability.

03

Finish your move

Attack with your units, use your hero power and reduce the opposing hero’s 30 health to zero.

What is available today?

The test build includes turn-based AI duels, a card collection, deck editing and an introductory story. Card balance and interface improvements are ongoing.

Local play without an account is available. Google and Apple sign-in are not yet enabled; progress stays on this device. Online multiplayer and cross-device saves are not available.

There is no general store release yet. This page does not offer an APK download or a purchase.
