Uygulamanın resmi kaynak kodundaki PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy manifest dosyası uyarınca:

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 12, 2026 • Effective Date: October 12, 2026

Key Summary: Nexus: Card Duel & Deckbuilder holds an Apple-verified "Data Not Collected" declaration (NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes: []). There is zero user tracking (NSPrivacyTracking: false). No camera, microphone, GPS, or contact permissions are required. Your deck setups and progress remain 100% on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how data and permissions are handled when using Nexus: Card Duel & Deckbuilder (Original Series: Nexus: Oathbound Realms, Bundle Identifier: com.codexa.nexus, Apple ID: 6822046146), developed by Erdinç Topal (CodExa).

1. Apple Privacy Nutrition Label Declaration

In strict compliance with Apple's PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy manifest requirements:

  • Collected Data Types: NONE (NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes: []). No personal names, locations, photos, or contact information are collected or uploaded.

Nexus respects player privacy by requesting zero intrusive device permissions:

Under Apple's privacy manifest framework, the app accesses NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryUserDefaults:

All single-player modes (Rogue Expedition, Tactical Puzzles, and AI Practice) are built to function 100% offline without any cellular data or Wi-Fi connection. Internet access is only required for Apple StoreKit in-app purchases and optional reward chests.
