# Privacy Policy for Classtings

**Effective date:** July 10, 2026  
**Developer:** CodExa  
**Contact:** [erdinctopal.et@gmail.com](mailto:erdinctopal.et@gmail.com)  

---

### The short version
Classtings is an offline-first teacher assistant app that secure-syncs data to the cloud (Google Firebase) once connected. We enforce strict data ownership (all student and class records belong to you, the teacher) and never share or monetize any data. We comply with COPPA, FERPA, and KVKK requirements to ensure a safe digital classroom environment.

---

### 1. Data Ownership & Security
All records, student profiles, pedagogical notes, and performance evaluations entered into Classtings belong exclusively to the teacher who created them. We do not sell, rent, or share this data with advertising networks or any third parties. 

All cloud synchronization with Firebase Firestore uses end-to-end encryption in transit (TLS) and encryption at rest, hosted on secure Google Cloud servers.

### 2. Information We Collect and Process
To provide our synchronization and ZPD (Zone of Proximal Development) analytics services, Classtings processes:
* **Account Info:** Sign-in details processed securely via Google Identity Service & Credential Manager (Firebase Authentication).
* **Classroom Data:** Student rosters, attendance records, scoring, and performance inputs.
* **Teacher Notes:** Both public student feedback and strategic/private notes. Private notes are strictly invisible to students or veli panels and are encrypted.
* **Audio Transcriptions:** Voice notes are transcribed on-device (where supported) or securely sent via Google Speech APIs. The audio files are not stored on our servers.

### 3. COPPA, FERPA, and KVKK Compliance
Classtings is built with educational regulations in mind:
* **FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act):** Classtings acts as a secure school service provider under the direct control of the teacher/school.
* **COPPA & GDPR-K:** Classtings does not directly market to or collect personal data from children under 13 without teacher/parent authorization. Student accounts (future vision) require 6-digit classroom codes generated and managed solely by the teacher.
* **KVKK (Turkey Data Protection Law):** All data processing is done based on the teacher's consent and classroom administration duties. Contact [erdinctopal.et@gmail.com](mailto:erdinctopal.et@gmail.com) for KVKK requests.

### 4. Controlled Visibility
Any data marked as "Private" (e.g., strategic classroom plans, financial reminders, personal logs) by the teacher is flagged in Firestore and will never be shared with student panels or exported in student-facing PDF performance reports.

### 5. Your Right to Deletion
You maintain complete control over your data. At any time, you can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated classroom/student records from the Firebase database directly via the app settings or by contacting us.

### 6. Changes to this Policy
We will notify users of any updates by posting the new policy on this page. Major updates will also be highlighted in the app's release notes.
