iOS & Android • Financial Mathematics & Probability Strategy

Invest in expected value, not luck.

Compoundia turns portfolio management into a tactile financial mathematics board game powered by 2d6 probability distributions, live compound interest curves, and 3×3 magic-square matrix bingo.

FV = PV × (1 + r/n)ⁿᵗ · E[R] = Σ Pᵢ × Yieldᵢ
  • 2d6 Probability Heatmap: Compare exact 1/36 to 6/36 dice odds across 12 sectors and calculate live expected return per turn.
  • Compound Interest & Leverage Lab: Experiment with principal, turn rate, and horizon sliders to visualize exponential growth vs. debt service.
  • 3×3 Magic Matrix Bingo: Unlock financial milestones arranged in a Lo Shu magic square; complete rows, columns, or diagonals for compounding dividend bonuses.
  • 6 Campaign Scenarios + Sandbox Ledger: Progress from First Ledger to Rate Hike, Liquidity Crunch, and Quant Final.
  • Zero gambling or real money: Purely educational financial literacy and quantitative strategy playable offline.
